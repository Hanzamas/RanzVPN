Ranz VPN: third-party binaries that the node loads at run time
=============================================================

The files below are not in this repository. CI fetches each one, pinned by its version and its hash, and checks its
signature; ONE-3 (#312) carries them inside ranz.exe as Payloads. Each is used under the terms of its own licence.
This file records those terms and the rules that a redistribution of the file follows (the NOTICE rules). A new
version changes the pin here and in its fetch script together.


Wintun 0.14.1
-------------

What:    wintun.dll, the L3 TUN adapter of WireGuard LLC (https://www.wintun.net), which the client role of the node
         loads from the directory of its executable (src/Cedar/NodeClient.c and NodeWintun.c; CORE-S3-1, #71).

Pinned:  https://www.wintun.net/builds/wintun-0.14.1.zip
         SHA-256 07c256185d6ee3652e09fa55c0b673e2624b565e02c4b9091c79ca7d2f24ef51, the hash that wintun.net publishes
         (.github/scripts/fetch-wintun.ps1 checks it). The node takes wintun/bin/amd64/wintun.dll on x64 and
         wintun/bin/arm64/wintun.dll on arm64, each signed by WireGuard LLC (Authenticode), which the script checks too.
