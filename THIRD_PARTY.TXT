Ranz VPN: third-party components that ranz.exe and the node carry
==================================================================

Each component below is used under the terms of its own licence. This file records those terms, the pin of
each component and the rules that a redistribution follows (the NOTICE rules). A new version changes the pin
here and where the component is pinned (go.mod and go.sum for a Go library, the fetch script for a binary)
together. The licence texts themselves are in the NOTICE inside ranz.exe (controller/cmd/ranz/NOTICE, printed
by `ranz notice`).


REALITY (github.com/xtls/reality)
---------------------------------

What:    the server side of the REALITY transport, a fork of the crypto/tls package of Go that answers a client
         with the proof of the server key as a TLS 1.3 server and splices every other connection to a real
         site. `ranz mimic`, the mimicry sidecar of a Node, links it as a library (controller/internal/mimic/
         server.go; CORE-P2-1, #85, ADR-0023). The library has no client side: the client of the protocol is
         controller/internal/mimic/realityclient.go, on uTLS.

Pinned:  github.com/xtls/reality v0.0.0-20261014101318-83f621788ca8, the commit 83f621788ca8 of
         https://github.com/XTLS/REALITY (go.mod, and the hashes of go.sum, which `go mod verify` checks in the
         supply-chain workflow). The sidecar uses the library as the module has it: no replace directive, no
         copy, no patch.

Licence: the Mozilla Public License, Version 2.0 (the file LICENSE of the repository). It is file-level
         copyleft: the files of REALITY stay under it, and the files of Ranz VPN, which only call it, are not
         Covered Software (section 3.3). In short:
         - a recipient of ranz.exe must be told that ranz.exe carries REALITY, under which licence, and where
           the source of the version that ranz.exe carries is (section 3.2(a)): the NOTICE says so, with the
           address of the repository and the commit;
         - a file of REALITY that Ranz VPN modifies must be published under the same licence (section 3.1);
         - the licence notices of its files are never removed (section 3.4);
         - it comes without warranty and without liability.
         The part of it that is forked from the crypto/tls package of Go is under the BSD 3-Clause licence of
         the Go authors as well (the file LICENSE-Go of the repository), which is the text of uTLS below.


uTLS (github.com/refraction-networking/utls)
--------------------------------------------

What:    the TLS client library that sends the ClientHello of a browser. The client role of `ranz mimic` makes
         the ClientHello of Edge (by default), Chrome or Firefox with it (controller/internal/mimic/
         fingerprint.go and realityclient.go; CORE-P2-1, #85).

Pinned:  github.com/refraction-networking/utls v1.8.2, a release, never a branch or a pseudo-version
         (go.mod, go.sum). The pin is bumped for each major release of the browsers: the parrots go stale
         otherwise, so .github/workflows/mimic-pin.yml runs on a schedule and fails when the review of the
         parrots is older than six weeks or uTLS has a newer release or a newer parrot (.github/scripts/
         mimic-pin.py); a bump changes go.mod, go.sum, utlsPin of fingerprint_test.go and the day of the
         review in fingerprint.go together.

Licence: the BSD 3-Clause licence of the Go authors (the file LICENSE of the repository). In short:
         - the copyright notice, the list of conditions and the disclaimer are reproduced with every binary
           that carries it: the NOTICE has them;
         - the names of Google Inc. and of the contributors may not endorse or promote a product without
           written permission;
         - it comes without warranty and without liability.


Libraries that REALITY and uTLS bring with them
-----------------------------------------------

What:    github.com/juju/ratelimit v1.0.2, which REALITY imports for the rate limit of its fallback
         connections (a limit that `ranz mimic` never sets); github.com/andybalholm/brotli v1.2.3 (MIT) and
         github.com/klauspost/compress v1.20.1 (BSD 3-Clause, with parts under Apache-2.0 and MIT), which uTLS
         uses for the compression of certificates; github.com/pires/go-proxyproto v0.15.0 (Apache-2.0), which
         REALITY imports for the PROXY protocol (a protocol that `ranz mimic` never turns on); and
         golang.org/x/crypto (BSD 3-Clause, the Go authors), whose acme and autocert packages get the
         certificate of the own decoy site. The SBOM of the supply-chain workflow names every module of
         go.mod at its version.

Licence: all of them are permissive, except github.com/juju/ratelimit: the GNU Lesser General Public
         License, version 3, with a special exception (the file LICENSE of the repository). Because ranz.exe
         links it statically, ranz.exe is a Combined Work in the sense of that licence. The exception lets
         Ranz VPN convey it without the Minimal Corresponding Source and the installation information of
         sections 4d and 4e, provided that it complies with the other provisions. So:
         - the NOTICE says prominently that the library is used and covered by the licence (section 4a), and
           holds the text of the licence of the library, word for word, with a reference to the GNU General
           Public License, version 3, which that text calls the GNU GPL (section 4b);
         - the terms under which ranz.exe is conveyed must, taken together, effectively not restrict the
           modification of the portions of the library that ranz.exe contains, nor the reverse engineering
           for debugging such modifications (the first paragraph of section 4). OPEN DECISION for the
           maintainer: the licence of Ranz VPN (ADR-0036) and the self-integrity check and the anti-debug
           measures of the release variant (ADR-0036 on anti-debug, SEC-3) must be read against that
           sentence before a release that carries `ranz mimic`; the alternatives are a licence that carves
           the library out, or a REALITY without that import, which is a fork whose modified files are then
           published under the Mozilla Public License.

NOTICE rules for every package that carries ranz.exe with `ranz mimic` (ONE-2 and later):
         1. The NOTICE of the package holds the licence texts above word for word. The test
            TestNoticeHasTheLicencesOfTheMimicryLibraries compares them with the files of the modules that
            the build uses, so a new version of a library whose text changed fails until the NOTICE follows.
         2. The libraries ship as they are in the module cache of the pinned version: never patched,
            never copied into the tree. A patched REALITY would have to publish its modified files.
         3. The sidecar is a process of its own that the Node starts (`ranz.exe mimic -pipe NAME`), and it
            links nothing of the Node: the named pipe is the boundary (ADR-0023).
         4. Ranz VPN names REALITY, uTLS and the browsers whose ClientHello it sends only to say what it
            carries and what it sends, never to promote itself.
