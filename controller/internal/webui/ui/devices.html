Devices

The WireGuard Devices of the Intent: phones, laptops and routers that join one Network over standard WireGuard through their gateway Node, with the last handshake and the traffic that the gateway reported for each.

Make a Device


The controller makes the key pair of the Device and a PresharedKey, adds the Device to the Intent, and shows its finished configuration and the QR code of it once: it keeps no private key. A router or an advanced user that holds its own key gives the public key, and the controller then makes the PresharedKey alone.
