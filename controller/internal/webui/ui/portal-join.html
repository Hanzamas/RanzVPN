Join tokens

A join token enrols one device of yours that cannot sign in itself, such as a server without a browser. Make it here and give its secret to the device, which presents it with its static key: the device becomes one of your devices, in your networks. A token works once and ends after a day, and the controller keeps only a hash of its secret, so the secret shows once, when the token is made.
